Here are 8 candidate taglines for **Maru**, each with a distinct intent:

1. **“From tide to tread — wear the sea back.”**  
Intent: Sustainability — transforms ocean waste into a wearable act of restoration.

2. **“Your sole has a soul. It came from the sea.”**  
Intent: Emotional brand identity — connects the wearer to the shoes’ deeper purpose.

3. **“One circle. One ocean. Every step closes the loop.”**  
Intent: Brand identity — uses “Maru” (circle) to symbolize circularity and completion.

4. **“Walk on what the sea gave back.”**  
Intent: Sustainability — short, poetic, and reframes recycled plastic as a gift, not garbage.

5. **“Cushioned by the coast, built for the city.”**  
Intent: Comfort + style — bridges marine materials with urban functionality.

6. **“Trash becomes tread. Your feet become the tide.”**  
Intent: Wordplay + brand identity — creates a rhythmic transformation arc that feels personal.

7. **“Step lighter. Leave a cleaner wake.”**  
Intent: Minimalist emotional appeal — addresses both physical comfort and environmental footprint.

8. **“Zero waste. One Maru. Infinite tide.”**  
Intent: Brand identity + sustainability — uses rhythm and the circle/zero metaphor to create a compact, repeatable slogan.
