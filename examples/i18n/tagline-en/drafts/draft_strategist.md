Here are 5–10 tagline candidates for **Maru**, each with its strategic intent:

1. **“Step inside the circle that gives back to the sea.”**  
   Intent: Brand identity + sustainability — plays on “Maru” (circle/round) and closing the loop.

2. **“Carry the coast with every step.”**  
   Intent: Sustainability + emotional appeal — makes the recycled origin tangible and personal.

3. **“From shoreline to skyline.”**  
   Intent: Brand identity + urban lifestyle — captures the journey from coastal waste to city streets.

4. **“Wear the wave of change.”**  
   Intent: Sustainability + style — turns environmental action into a fashion statement.

5. **“Your feet, the sea’s shield.”**  
   Intent: Sustainability + brand identity — short, poetic, and positions the wearer as a protector.

6. **“Clean ocean. Clean streets. Clean kicks.”**  
   Intent: Style + sustainability — rhythmic, streetwear-friendly, and memorable.

7. **“The circle that cleans the coast.”**  
   Intent: Brand identity + sustainability — direct wordplay on “Maru” and circular economy.

8. **“Run with the tide, not against it.”**  
   Intent: Comfort + sustainability — resonates with active urban users while implying effortless eco-consciousness.
