# Output format (OutputFormat)

- Deliverable type: tagline
- Description: A short, memorable phrase for the sneaker brand 'Maru' that highlights its recycled-material construction and appeals to eco-conscious consumers.
- Output length range: 10–200 chars
- Deliverable itself: yes (output_is_direct)

## Draft format guidance (draft_guidance)

Generate 5-10 candidate taglines for Maru. For each candidate, note the intent: whether it focuses on sustainability, style, comfort, or brand identity. Use wordplay, rhythm, and emotional appeal. Keep candidates under 15 words each.

## Finalization format guidance (finalize_guidance)

Select the strongest candidate and refine it. The final artifact must be a single tagline, presented as a standalone line of text. It should be concise (preferably 5-8 words), easy to say, and clearly tie the recycled material to the Maru brand's value proposition. Avoid clichés and ensure it works in marketing contexts such as packaging, ads, or social media.
