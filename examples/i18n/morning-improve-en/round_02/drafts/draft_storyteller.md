Overall intent: one sentence you can retell to anyone — **No, Yes, Win, Open** — so the day becomes your story, not an interruption.

- 0:00–0:15 | 15 min | **No** — water, sit, no phone. Benefit: the cut; you narrate, not are narrated.
- 0:15–0:25 | 10 min | **Yes** — daylight, move. Benefit: consent to being a body; effort and alertness rise.
- 0:25–0:40 | 15 min | **Care** — shower, dress, slow breakfast. Benefit: communion; you inhabit yourself, not refuel.
- 0:40–0:55 | 15 min | **Win** — write the feared task, then “If all else fails, this is the win.” Benefit: dread becomes a chosen battle.
- 0:55–1:10 | 15 min | **Open** — messages, reading, out the door. Benefit: the world enters after you speak, not before.

Retold as: “I wrote the thing I was avoiding before breakfast.” That seed travels.
