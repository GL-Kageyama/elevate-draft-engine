**Overall intent:** Start with physiological wake-up, then mental clarity and intentional planning, so the day begins from agency rather than reaction.

- **6:30 – 6:35 (5 min) Hydration + light exposure:** Drink water, step outside or sit by a window. Resets circadian rhythm and rehydrates the brain.
- **6:35 – 6:50 (15 min) Movement:** Light stretching, yoga, or a brisk walk. Increases blood flow and alertness without exhausting willpower.
- **6:50 – 7:00 (10 min) Mindfulness:** Breath focus or short meditation. Lowers reactive stress and builds attention control.
- **7:00 – 7:15 (15 min) Planning:** Write the day's top 3 priorities and one key outcome. Turns vague intention into executable direction.
- **7:15 – 7:30 (15 min) Learning:** Read, listen, or review notes. Stretches the mind before the day's demands crowd it out.
