Core deviation: A productive morning is not a buffet of positive habits; it is a quarantine against the world and a voluntary encounter with discomfort.

- **6:30–6:50 (20 min) “No-input zone”** — Sit in dimness with no phone, no text, no music. Benefit: stops other people’s emergencies from hijacking your brain before your feet hit the floor.
- **6:50–7:00 (5 min) “Cold water/shower”** — Not a spa ritual, but deliberate discomfort. Benefit: you endure the first unpleasant thing by choice, so the rest of the day feels comparatively easy.
- **7:00–7:20 (20 min) “One ugly paragraph”** — Write the task you are avoiding, in one messy, unpolished paragraph. Benefit: converts vague dread into a measurable next action — more useful than grateful affirmations.
- **7:20–7:25 (5 min) “Single outcome pick”** — One sentence: “If everything else fails, this is the win.” Benefit: scarcity forces real prioritization; a ten-item plan is a plan to ignore everything.
- **7:25–7:45 (20 min) “Brisk outside walk until breathless”** — Includes daylight exposure. Benefit: anchors circadian rhythm and moves blood without performative wellness theater.

Overall intent: start with a quarantine from input and a small voluntary shock, so the day’s first real choice is yours — not your notifications’.
