**Candidate Morning Routine (adjust to your own rhythm—this is a starting point, not a mandate)**

- **6:30–6:35 (5 min) — Hydration**  
  Drink a glass of water before anything else. Rehydrates after sleep and gives a small, gentle anchor to the day.

- **6:35–6:50 (15 min) — Light exposure + gentle movement**  
  Step outside or sit by a window with natural light; do slow stretches. Tells your body the day has started and grounds you in your physical self.

- **6:50–7:00 (10 min) — Mindfulness / breathing**  
  Sit with whatever is present—no forced positivity. Notices your inner weather and creates a gap between feeling and reacting.

- **7:00–7:20 (20 min) — Planning + learning**  
  Choose one meaningful task for the day; read or listen to something that expands your mind. Intentional direction, not a to-do list dump.

- **7:20–7:30 (10 min) — Breakfast / transition**  
  Eat something real, no phone. Carries nourishment and attention into the rest of the morning.

**Overall intent:** Start with clarity and calm before engaging with the day—not to optimize every minute, but to remember you are a human being, not a machine.
