**Overall intent:** Start with clarity and calm before engaging with the day.

- **6:00–6:05 — Hydration:** Drink a full glass of water. Rehydrates the brain and body after sleep, sharpening focus.
- **6:05–6:15 — Light exposure:** Step outside or sit by a window. Regulates circadian rhythm, boosting alertness and mood.
- **6:15–6:35 — Movement:** Stretch, walk, or do light strength work. Wakes the body, increases blood flow, and releases tension.
- **6:35–6:45 — Mindfulness:** Meditate or breathe deeply for 10 minutes. Reduces reactivity and anchors attention.
- **6:45–6:55 — Planning:** Review top 3 priorities for the day. Creates direction and prevents decision fatigue.
- **6:55–7:00 — Learning:** Read or listen to something valuable. Stimulates curiosity and sets an upward mental tone.
