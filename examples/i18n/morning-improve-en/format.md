# Output format (OutputFormat)

- Deliverable type: morning routine plan
- Description: A structured morning routine that pairs timed activities with a grounded mindset to set up a productive day.
- Output length range: 600–4000 chars
- Deliverable itself: yes (output_is_direct)

## Draft format guidance (draft_guidance)

Draft a list of candidate morning activities (e.g., hydration, movement, mindfulness, planning, learning, light exposure) with approximate time slots, the duration of each, and a note on the benefit it provides. Add one sentence stating the overall intent of the routine, such as 'start with clarity and calm before engaging with the day.'

## Finalization format guidance (finalize_guidance)

Produce a final artifact with a title, a short philosophy paragraph, a step-by-step schedule from wake-up to start of work (each step has a duration, the activity, and its purpose), and a final 'Adapting This Routine' section with tips for busy days, weekends, or individual preferences. Ensure the routine has a coherent arc that balances grounding (calm, presence, self-care) and productivity (planning, focus, momentum).
