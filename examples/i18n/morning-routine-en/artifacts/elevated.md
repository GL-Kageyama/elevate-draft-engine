## The Threshold Rite — 30–45 Minutes

**Overview**  
Start between 6:00 and 7:30. Core intention: cross from sleep into the world as the author of your day, not its first responder.

**The Routine**

1. **Wake** *(0:00–1:00)* — Sit up, place both feet on the floor, leave your phone untouched. You are leaving sleep; do not carry its fog into the day.

2. **Water** *(1:00–3:00)* — Drink 500 ml of water near a window or bright lamp. Hydrate your body and signal to your brain that the day has begun.

3. **Breathe** *(3:00–6:00)* — Breathe in for 4, hold for 4, out for 4, hold for 4. Repeat for 3 minutes. This is the gap between who you were and who you are becoming.

4. **Move** *(6:00–12:00)* — Stretch your spine, hips, and shoulders for 5–10 minutes. Let your body cross the threshold with you.

5. **Reflect** *(12:00–17:00)* — Write three lines: one gratitude, one insight, one intention. Choose the kind of day you are making before the world chooses for you.

6. **Plan** *(17:00–22:00)* — Write your top three tasks for the day. Mark the hardest one. Decide when it starts. Commit with one sentence: *“I begin with…”*

7. **Nourish** *(22:00–30:00)* — Eat a meal with protein and slow carbohydrates, without screens. You are a person first; the inbox can wait.

**Grounding Elements**  
Keep your phone away until after your first chosen act. After movement, take one deliberate minute of uselessness: stare out the window, do nothing, produce nothing. Let your first sip of water be slow. These small pauses make the whole morning a sanctuary.

**Productivity Tools**  
Limit yourself to three priorities. Schedule the hardest task first. If you cannot choose, decide by coin toss or roll of a die — bind the choice, then move on. A chosen plan beats a perfect plan.

**Make It Work**  
On busy days, use the 10-minute version: water, one breath, one line of intention, one chosen act. If you start late, begin at the current step—never chase the sequence. If you skip a step, release the guilt. The rite is complete the moment you enter the day with an act you chose, not an inbox you answered.
