### Morning Routine: 6:30–7:45

**6:30–6:35 — Wake & Hydrate**  
Drink 500ml water, stand in daylight. No phone yet.  
Purpose: reset circadian clock, rehydrate.  
Transition cue: glass empty → go to movement.

**6:35–6:50 — Movement**  
10–15 min brisk walk, stretch, or bodyweight reps.  
Purpose: build energy and physical momentum.  
Transition cue: breath steady → go to reflection.

**6:50–7:05 — Reflection**  
Journal or sit quietly. Write 3 gratitudes + 1 intention.  
Purpose: ground attention, reduce autopilot.  
Transition cue: pen down → go to planning.

**7:05–7:20 — Planning**  
Choose top 3 tasks; schedule the hardest one first.  
Purpose: turn intention into execution.  
Transition cue: calendar open → go to meaningful start.

**7:20–7:45 — Meaningful Start**  
Slow breakfast, one page of a book, or a short call with someone you care about.  
Purpose: begin from agency, not inboxes.  
Transition cue: finish eating → start work.

**Optional variations**  
- Low energy: skip movement, do 5-minute breathing, keep only one priority.  
- Short on time: hydrate + one intention, 10-minute walk, top-3 plan.
