**THESIS — Morning self-optimization is the day's first tax. A productive day isn't earned; it's entered. So: zero decisions before work-start, plus one ritual that does nothing.**

**Wake 6:30–9:00:**
- **6:30–6:45 Awake, not unparked.** Water, dim light, no phone. Cue: glass empty.
- **6:45–7:30 Useless Hour.** Same coffee, cold; sit and stew. Not meditation — boredom grounds. Cue: the cold sip.
- **7:30–7:45 One Decision.** Pick one task from last night's list of 3; place a coin on the keyboard. Cue: coin lands.
- **7:45–8:30 Walk the dog** (imaginary counts). No audio. Proprioception, not cardio. Cue: door closes.
- **8:30–9:00 Same breakfast, then the shove.** First keystroke — no email. Cue: you typed.

**Anchors:** hydration, boring movement, useless reflection, single-coin planning, committed start.

**Variations:** Dead day — skip everything, keep the coin. Rush day — glass, brew, coin, keystroke (30 min). The ritual is immunity, not merit.
