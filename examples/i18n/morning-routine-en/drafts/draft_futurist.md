**6:30–6:35 — Wake & Light**  
No phone. Open curtains, stand by window. Purpose: fix circadian anchor. Transition: drink a full glass of water.

**6:35–6:45 — Hydration + Movement**  
Water with electrolytes; 10 minutes of mobility or light exercise. Purpose: wake the body, shake off stiffness. Transition: feet planted, move to a quiet spot.

**6:45–7:00 — Reflection**  
Journal three lines: what I’m grateful for, my intention, one obstacle I expect. Purpose: ground the mind before action. Transition: close the notebook, open the planner.

**7:00–7:15 — Planning**  
Choose top three tasks; time-block the first one specifically. Purpose: turn intention into direction. Transition: stand up, step away from the desk.

**7:15–7:30 — Meaningful Start**  
Do 15 minutes of your most important work, or read a short passage that centers you. Purpose: earn a win before the world demands noise. Transition: breakfast, commute, or official start.

**Optional variations**  
Low energy: cut movement to 5 minutes, skip journaling, keep planning. Late start: compress reflection into the commute; do meaningful start as your first meeting prep. High energy: swap the reading for a short run or harder exercise.
