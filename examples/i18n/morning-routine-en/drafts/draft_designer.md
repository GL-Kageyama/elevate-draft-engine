Core thesis: A morning should be a descent into presence, not a checklist. Grounding is the foundation; productivity is the clear sky that appears when attention settles. Let each transition be carried by a sensory cue, so the whole moves as one continuous breath.

Evidence:
- 0–10 min: water at the window, feet bare on cold wood (grounding); the glass’s chill against your chest — transition when your gaze shifts from horizon to room.
- 10–25 min: slow floor stretch (grounding); reach for the light pooling on the floor — transition on a long exhale as you stand.
- 25–55 min: one journal page, one sentence of intention, then choose the first meaningful task (grounding into productive); close the notebook — its latch click is your starting gate.

Premise: If energy is low or time is short, compress to 10 minutes — water, one stretch, one line — but never reorder the thresholds; the shell preserves the ritual.
