**Morning routine (45 min):**

- **0–5 Wake:** Drink water, get sunlight. Purpose: hydrate, set circadian cue. Transition: phone away.
- **5–15 Move:** Stretch or brisk walk. Purpose: wake body, circulate. Transition: three slow breaths.
- **15–25 Reflect:** Write 3 gratitudes + one intention. Purpose: ground. Transition: close notebook.
- **25–35 Plan:** Pick top 3 tasks, assign time blocks. Purpose: orient. Transition: open first task.
- **35–45 Start:** Execute hardest/deepest task. Purpose: momentum. Transition: begin work.

**Variations:** Low energy — replace movement with shower, cut reflection to one line, keep hydration and one priority. Late start — merge reflection and planning into 10 minutes, extend start block to 25.
