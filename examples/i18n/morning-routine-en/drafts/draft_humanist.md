**Morning routine** (wake-up → start of day)  
- **0–10 min · Wake & Hydrate** — Eyes open, water before phone. Purpose: grounding; reentry into the body. Transition cue: glass empty, feet on floor.  
- **10–30 min · Movement** — Stretch, walk, or 10 sun salutations. Purpose: productive energy; shake off sleep. Transition cue: breath deepens, heart rate rises.  
- **30–45 min · Reflection** — Sit with coffee/tea, 3 slow breaths, one intention. Purpose: grounding; orient the heart before the mind. Transition cue: intention spoken aloud.  
- **45–60 min · Plan** — Write top 3 tasks, one meaningful priority. Purpose: productive clarity. Transition cue: pen down, notebook closed.  
- **60–75 min · Meaningful Start** — Pour energy into the most human work: a craft, reading, a conversation, or a slow breakfast. Purpose: productive *and* grounding; you begin the day as yourself, not as a response machine. Transition cue: clear work boundary — "I am ready."

**Variations**  
- *Short schedule:* compress to 30 min — hydrate, one movement, one task.  
- *Low energy:* swap intense movement for gentle stretching; skip planning, keep intention.  
- *Mid-day start:* repeat the same arc before work, compressed to 45 min.
