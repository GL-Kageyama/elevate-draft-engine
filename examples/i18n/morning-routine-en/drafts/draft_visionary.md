**Core thesis:** A morning routine works not by optimizing output but by institutionalizing a gap between sleep and service. Its productivity is the ability to enter the day as a subject, not as a resource; its grounding is remembering you exist before you execute.

**Evidence:**
- 0:00–0:25 **Interruption + ownership** — no phone, cold water, slow movement; grounding, cue: breath settles.
- 0:25–0:50 **Boundary + choice** — one refusal, plan three external demands and one internal anchor; productive, cue: notebook closed.
- 0:50–1:00 **Presence** — tea without ingestion; meaningful start, cue: tea finished. Start work only after this.

**Variations:** low energy — replace movement with lying breathing; rushed — compress to 20 minutes, keep interruption and tea.

**Premise:** The day’s default is servitude; routine is a declaration of independence.
