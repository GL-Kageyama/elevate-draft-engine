**Morning Routine: The Story of a Day You Chose**

**6:30 Wake** – Open curtains, no phone. Purpose: meet the day before it meets you. Transition: drink the water left by your bed.

**6:35 Hydrate + Land** – 500ml water, feet on floor, 10 slow breaths. Purpose: signal safety, turn off sleep alarm. Transition: body present → move.

**6:45 Move** – 10 min stretch or brisk walk. Purpose: wake circulation, shake off stagnation. Transition: breathing deepens → reflect.

**7:00 Reflect** – Write three lines: gratitude, intention, worry to release. Purpose: ground emotion, choose a lens. Transition: close notebook → plan.

**7:15 Plan** – Pick top 3 tasks; time-block the first. Purpose: point attention at what matters. Transition: place one object on desk as anchor → start.

**7:30 Meaningful start** – 25 min on hardest task before email. Purpose: earn the day, don’t react to it. Transition: now you carry momentum into work.

**7:55 Work begins** – Review calendar; answer only urgent. Purpose: protect the plan you chose.

**Variations**  
Low energy: skip worry line, do 5 min movement, keep only a 15-minute meaningful start.  
Late wake: compress to hydration, one intention, one 15-minute deep task.
