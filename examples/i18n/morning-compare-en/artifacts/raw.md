**Core Principle:** A productive day starts with a grounded nervous system, not a rushed to-do list.

## The Routine

**6:30–6:40 — Hydrate & Sunlight**  
*Purpose:* Wake the body and set your circadian rhythm.  
*Why it works:* Water rehydrates after sleep, and morning light triggers cortisol release that boosts alertness and regulates sleep later.

**6:40–7:00 — Mindfulness**  
*Purpose:* Calm mental noise and set an intentional tone for the day.  
*Why it works:* A short meditation, breathing exercise, or journaling lowers reactivity and helps you respond rather than react to stress.

**7:00–7:30 — Movement**  
*Purpose:* Release tension and energize the body before mental work.  
*Why it works:* Movement increases blood flow, endorphins, and oxygen to the brain, improving focus and mood for hours.

**7:30–8:00 — Planning & Priorities**  
*Purpose:* Review your calendar and choose your top 1–3 outcomes for the day.  
*Why it works:* Clarity reduces decision fatigue and ensures your energy goes where it matters most.

**8:00–8:30 — Breakfast**  
*Purpose:* Fuel your body and brain with protein, fiber, and hydration.  
*Why it works:* Stable glucose and enough water prevent mid-morning crashes and cravings.

**8:30 — Start Work**  
*Purpose:* Begin with your highest-impact task.  
*Why it works:* Willpower is strongest in the morning; completing a meaningful task first builds momentum and confidence.

## Adaptations

- **Busy day:** Compress to 20 minutes — hydrate, 5-minute mindfulness, 10-minute walk, and choose your one priority while getting dressed or commuting.
- **Weekend:** If you sleep in, keep the *sequence* not the clock: hydrate → sunlight → slow movement → breakfast → then plan your day. Make it relaxed, not rigid.
- **Zero energy day:** Sit by a window for 5 minutes, drink water, stretch for 10 minutes, and write down one thing you’ll complete.

## Non-negotiables

1. Drink water within 15 minutes of waking.  
2. Get at least 5 minutes of daylight or bright light.  
3. Decide your one priority for the day before you start any work.
