**Core thesis:** A productive morning isn't a machine-start; it is self-reconciliation. Hear what’s true in you first, then protect one meaningful action.

**Grounding candidates:**
- Presence sip (5 min): tea, no screens — arrive, not perform.
- Real journal (5 min): write what’s actually there — name the truth.
- Tender stretch (5 min): neck/shoulders — inhabit your body.
- One-word check (1 min): "What do I need?" — honor your own voice.

**Productivity candidates:**
- One chosen task (5 min): pick one meaningful task — direction without overwhelm.
- Deep work block (45–90 min): before messages — protect agency.
- Walk outdoors (15 min): daylight/rhythm — reset perspective.
- Boundary sentence (2 min): "Today I decline…" — sustainable energy.

**Orderings:**
1. *Human First:* Real journal → stretch → chosen task → deep work. Buffer 15 min.
2. *Doer:* Walk → presence sip → deep work → boundary. Buffer 10.
3. *Slow Starter:* Presence sip → stretch → read → chosen task; deep work after breakfast. Buffer 20.
